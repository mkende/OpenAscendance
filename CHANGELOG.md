# Changelog

## [0.1.0-alpha.2] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- Options: music and sound effects volume sliders. Every setting applies as
  soon as it is picked; Cancel reverts them. The game starts in full screen
  by default, and Alt+Enter saves the full screen setting.
- System Display: a button to deselect the selected ship or planet; a click on
  the selected planet's picture opens its planet screen.
- Lists and the research tree scroll by finger drag and mouse wheel.
- Touch: a long press shows the help, holds a button, or acts as a right click
  where the screen uses one; three fingers or more brought together go back; a
  touch during a System Display effect skips it.
- Buttons that would do nothing are greyed: ABANDON, the planet panels of a
  foreign planet, HULL when no other hull fits the design, extinct species.

### Changed

- Easier picking: a click just beside a button acts on it, a star is picked
  from a little farther away, and the ships at a star pick that star.
- Smoother mouse pointer, kept inside the picture in full screen.
- Options: a more contrasted look for the selected choices; the opening menu
  has Options above Load Game, and no keyboard shortcuts on Android.

### Fixed

- Android: the sound comes back after a phone call or another app took the
  audio device.
- Android: the package has no `.apk.idsig` file any more.

## [0.1.0-alpha.1] - 2026-10-17

The first release: the complete game, Standard and Antagonizer variants.
